🤖 Mitty Verse Engineering

AI Robotic Hand Assembly (Python + Arduino)

Designed and built at Mitty Verse! I personally handled the complete physical assembly of the robotic hand — carefully stringing and routing internal mechanical threads (tendons) through each individual finger joint and anchoring them to high-torque servo motors! 🧵🖐️⚡️

Powered by a custom Python pipeline using OpenCV and CVZone (MediaPipe), the system tracks 21 hand landmarks on webcam, calculates finger gestures, and sends serial strings (e.g. $11111) to the Arduino board. The Arduino instantly actuates the servo motors, pulling the internal finger threads to recreate human hand movements live! 🤖💥

🧵 Thread/Tendon Mechanism 🔧 Mechanical Hand Assembly 🐍 Python ⚡️ Arduino C++ 📷 OpenCV 🖐️ CVZone / MediaPipe 🔌 Serial Protocol
